Pump Up is the human-in-the-loop layer for AI agents. When your agent needs an approval, a missing piece of information, or a handoff to a person, Pump Up routes the request to the right reviewer, enforces SLAs, and returns the answer to your agent — with a full audit trail of who decided what and why.

You add Pump Up to an existing agent in under ten lines. The rest — routing rules, queues, reviewers, escalations — is configured on the server, not hard-coded in your call site.

## How it fits

Generic AI agents can't be trusted to run sensitive workflows end-to-end. Real business processes have policies, thresholds, approval paths, and exception logic that need a human in the loop — not as a fallback, but as a deliberate control point.

Pump Up sits between your agent and the people who own those decisions. Your agent calls Pump Up, the right reviewer gets a focused ticket with the agent's context and proposed action, their decision flows back into your agent's run, and every step is logged for compliance and replay.
